AI Olympiad Society Philippines, Inc.

We organize national competitive programming, data science, and artificial intelligence competitions, selection trials, and educational programs. We select, train, mentor, and field official Philippine student delegations to international AI competitions, including the International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence (IOAI) and the Asia-Pacific Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence (APOAI). We advance youth education, research, and public capability in computer science, machine learning, and ethical AI, and work with educational institutions, government bodies, and scientific organizations to grow Philippine STEM talent.

A non-stock, non-profit organization registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of the Philippines.
